# Design System

Verdict uses a restrained editorial product system.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F7F7F5`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary surface: `#F0F1EF`
- Primary text: `#17191E`
- Secondary text: `#5C616B`
- Brand navy: `#192A44`
- Oxblood accent: `#8C2633`
- Focus: `#315E9E`

Navy is the main product colour. Oxblood is used sparingly for editorial accents and warnings. Gradients, glow effects and decorative blurred backgrounds are intentionally avoided.

## Typography

- Interface: Geist Sans
- Editorial headings: Source Serif 4
- Metrics: Geist Mono with tabular figures

Assessment passages use comfortable line length and line height. Full passages are not set in oversized bold type.

## Components

- Buttons use 6px radius and semantic variants.
- Panels use borders over shadows.
- Tables and rows are preferred for comparison and performance data.
- Cards are used only for self-contained surfaces.
- Icon-only controls require accessible names.

## Layout

The public marketing site, student application and admin surfaces are separated. The dashboard answers what to do next; it does not carry pricing cards, firm leaderboards or developer instructions.
