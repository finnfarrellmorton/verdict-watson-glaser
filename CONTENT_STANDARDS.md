# Content Standards

Every question must:

- Be original or licensed.
- Test one primary reasoning skill.
- Have one defensible answer.
- Avoid unnecessary specialist knowledge.
- Include an approved explanation.
- Include a reasoning rule and common trap.
- Track source type and review status.

Verdict must not copy official assessment questions, paid preparation questions, employer-owned materials or other platforms' question banks.

Verdict must not claim official norming or official percentile equivalence.
